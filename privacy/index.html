Privacy Policy for Learning Board

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

Learning Board ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website located at learningboard.online. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all users, including children, educators, and creators worldwide.

Because our core drawing application runs 100% client-side within your web browser, we do not collect, store, or share your artwork, notes, or personal account data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

Unlike traditional platforms, we do not require user accounts, email registrations, or profile creation.

  • No Server-Side Canvas Data: Your drawings, text, and voice interactions are processed entirely on your local device. No canvas data is transmitted to our servers.
  • No Direct Personal Data Collection: We do not directly collect names, email addresses (unless you voluntarily email us for support), or user profile information.

2. Local Storage and Data Persistence

To allow you to resume your work if you accidentally close your browser tab, this web tool utilizes your browser's native Local Storage or IndexedDB.

  • This data stays entirely on your computer or device.
  • We cannot access, view, or recover your saved local blackboard or whiteboard files.
  • You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's cache or using the "Clear All" function in the app.

3. Global Privacy Compliance

A. Children's Privacy (COPPA - USA)

Learning Board is designed to be safe for students and educators. We do not require accounts or knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

B. European Union & UK Users (GDPR)

For users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, Learning Board processes zero user canvas data on remote servers. For optional analytics and advertising cookies described below, you may accept or decline consent via our cookie banner and clear local browser storage at any time.

C. California Users (CCPA / CPRA - USA)

Learning Board does not directly sell personal information. Third-party advertising partners operating on our site may use cookies or device identifiers to deliver and measure advertisements; you may manage or opt out of such technologies using the controls described in Section 4.

4. Third-Party Services, Analytics, and Advertising

Hosting: Our website may be hosted using third-party static hosting platforms (such as GitHub Pages or Vercel). These hosting providers may automatically log basic network requests (such as your IP address) solely to deliver webpage files securely and prevent abuse.

Analytics (Google Tag Manager & Microsoft Clarity): We use analytics tools, including Google Tag Manager / Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, to understand general site traffic, page performance, and usability trends so we can improve the learning experience.

Advertising (Adsterra and Third-Party Ad Networks): To keep Learning Board completely free for educators and students, we display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks, including Adsterra (including display banners and interactive Social Bar ad units).

  • Adsterra and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, web beacons, pixels, or similar technologies to deliver relevant advertisements, limit ad frequency, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.
  • When ads load or are interacted with, third-party ad servers may automatically receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, approximate location (country/region), and referring URL.
  • You can manage your preferences via our cookie consent banner, disable or clear cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of interest-based advertising through industry opt-out portals such as www.aboutads.info/choices and optout.networkadvertising.org.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our tools, advertising partners, or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated timestamp.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Page or official repository channels.
